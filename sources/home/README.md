# The home set

Appliances, fixtures, hearths, utilities and vehicles of a house, **drawn by CadCraft** and shared under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): use them for anything, no credit needed.

Made by `tools/make-home.mjs` from `tools/home/` (no source files: the items are drawn in code). The geometry kit
(`kit.js`) builds closed, outward-facing solids from lofts, turned profiles, hollow basins, tubes and thin ribbons; each item
carries coloured parts (`CC-RENDER-PARTS`) so enamel, chrome, glass and rubber render as themselves.

## What is in it

- **Bath**: two-piece toilets (elongated, round-front), a one-piece skirted toilet, a wall-hung toilet, a bidet, a freestanding
  oval tub with floor filler, a clawfoot tub, a drop-in tub in a surround, a quarter-round corner shower, a vessel sink, a
  wall-hung lavatory, a 7 ft spa, a ceiling exhaust fan.
- **Kitchen and laundry**: gas and induction cooktops, single and double wall ovens, under-cabinet and island hoods,
  side-by-side, top-freezer and undercounter refrigerators, a chest freezer, a wine cooler, a farmhouse sink, a bar sink,
  a pull-down faucet, a disposer, a countertop microwave, a stacked washer and dryer, a top-load washer.
- **Hearth and utilities**: masonry and gas fireplaces, wood and pellet stoves, a fire pit, heat-pump and tankless water
  heaters, a water softener, a furnace, an AC condenser, a mini-split head, a panel radiator, a wall-hung boiler, a 200 A
  panel, a ceiling fan.
- **Vehicles**: sedan, SUV, pickup, minivan, hatchback, a motorcycle and a road bicycle.

## Conventions

Millimetres, Z up, the front faces −Y, the insertion point is the middle of the back at the floor (vehicles: the middle of the
footprint at the ground). Things that hang or sit on a counter carry their own height (`CC-MOUNT-HEIGHT`, mm). Sizes are
typical nominal sizes, not any maker's product; clearances in the descriptions are planning aids, not code distances.
